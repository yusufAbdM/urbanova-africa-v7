# Urbanova Africa V4: Legal intelligence audit

Legal & Regulatory Intelligence is displayed beneath **every** application route. It distinguishes Africa-wide treaties/policy frameworks, Nigerian federal sources, selected state agencies, and official source directories for Ghana, Kenya and South Africa.

**Limits:** The interface is a curated legal research directory, **not** a full 54-country legislative database, an authoritative legal opinion, or proof of regulatory compliance. The selected Nigeria states are Lagos, Ogun, Kano and Rivers, with a clearly labelled fallback for others. Source links point to institutions or treaty records and may require navigating to current legal instruments. Laws must be checked for enactment, amendments, territorial applicability and ratification before use. Regulatory content is not updated automatically.

**Follow-up engineering:** Verify each linked document with official gazettes; expand state and country coverage with versioned legal instrument metadata, commencement dates and provenance; add legal update monitoring and automated link checks; have a qualified local legal reviewer check any compliance recommendations.

**Privacy:** This feature does not collect or transmit the user's location or identity. Country and state selections are client-side UI state.
